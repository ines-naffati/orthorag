The XYZ Clavicle Plating System consists of pre-contoured titanium alloy (Ti-6Al-4V, ASTM F136) superior and anterior clavicle plates in left and right configurations, with 3.5 mm locking and non-locking cortical screws. The plates are provided sterile and non-sterile. The system is intended for fixation of fractures, malunions and nonunions of the clavicle.
