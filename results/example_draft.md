
## device_description
Device Description

The XYZ Clavicle Plating System consists of pre-contoured titanium alloy (Ti-6Al-4V, ASTM F136) superior and anterior clavicle plates in left and right configurations, with 3.5 mm locking and non-locking cortical screws [K250494]. The plates are provided sterile and non-sterile [K250494]. The system is intended for fixation of fractures, malunions, and nonunions of the clavicle [K250494].

The plates are fabricated from titanium alloy, which is a material commonly used in orthopedic implants due to its biocompatibility, corrosion resistance, and nontoxicity in a biological environment [K243308]. The 3.5 mm locking and non-locking cortical screws are also made from titanium alloy, which is consistent with the material used in other orthopedic devices [K243308].

The system is designed to provide fixation of clavicle fractures, malunions, and nonunions, which are common injuries in the shoulder and upper arm region [K232652]. The use of pre-contoured plates and screws allows for precise placement and fixation of the fracture site, promoting optimal healing and recovery [K232652].

The OsteoCentric Bone Plate and Screw System [K250494] includes additional screw sizes (2.1 mm, 2.4mm, and 2.7mm diameter).
